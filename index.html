How MeetBuddy works

Four steps. Thirty seconds to set up. Zero participants notified.

  1. 01

    Install the Chrome extension

    Add to Chrome in 30 seconds. No account required. Or run the desktop tray app for cross-platform capture with PipeWire.

  2. 02

    Join your meeting normally

    MeetBuddy runs silently via tabCapture and getUserMedia. No bot joins. No notification to other participants.

  3. 03

    AI processes the recording

    Faster-Whisper transcribes the audio locally. An LLM (Ollama, GPT-4o, or Claude) extracts summaries, decisions, and action items.

  4. 04

    Ask anything, anytime

    Semantic search powered by pgvector spans your entire meeting history. "What did the client say about budget?" — answered instantly.
